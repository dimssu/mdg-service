# Reel 03 · Credit & DOD — storyboard

**v2 — measured.** Every time below comes from the sound director's beat map
(`AUDIO.md`, `build/script.json`): voice word starts, and the board's flips,
each locked to its clack (`script.json → board`, a clack lands 0.11 s after
its flip starts). 120 BPM, 15 bars of 2 s. v1 (estimated) is in git history.

**Concept, in one line:** two questions on a departure board — how much, and by
when — that the board cannot answer until the picture lands in his chat; then
it answers both, counts the days down without dates, and folds into the call.

**Fixed geometry:** one axis x 540; titles at y 300 / 394; board rows centred (bars 5–8 at y 520 / 684, the payoff at 570 / 742 with the phone at 930),
640 px at rest (₹ + 6 digits at 76 × 136, 2 comma flaps at 30, gaps 6), at most
two at once; card window x 192–888. The board's flaps flip 0.03 s apart, the
clacks' own spacing.

**How a figure moves from the card to the board:** a white slip set in the
card's own type and text peels off the card while the card is standing still,
arcs up, and slides into its row's lower edge (the slot), clipped there, so it
never sits on the flaps; the row flips to it on its clack.

**Range:** kinetic type (hook, pain) · 3D (the flaps' turning halves, the board
folding into the bubble and rising out of it) · UI deconstruction (DUE AMOUNT,
DUE DATE and the card's own verdict lifted into the board; the tiles; the
card's bar) · data (`₹25,00,000 − ₹18,42,650 = ₹6,57,350`, the card's own 74% / 26% bar) · physical
(the tanker; the row goes to `0`, then `−`) · end card.

**Card framings:** each is set by its window edges, every edge falling between two lines of the card, and anchored at the window's top edge so the hold's push never moves the top cut; the bottom cut is proven to stay in its gap (`scene.js` throws if not). Between framings the window closes and reopens; the camera never pans across the card.

**Rules held:** no hard cut; no dated sequence; the window card never
changes; nothing fades over anything (rows fold, the sum wipes, slips keep
their backing); at most two board rows; the board never in red or green; no
clock; at most 7 Hindi words (measured); no still run of 0.5 s (measured).
