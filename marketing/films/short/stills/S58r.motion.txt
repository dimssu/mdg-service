The camera pushes in very slowly for the whole shot. Everyone stays still at their place with only small natural movements: breathing, a blink, a slight shift of weight. 3–6 s: the manager turns her head to look along at the attendants; the nearer attendant glances toward the road, then back. Hands stay where they are, at the sides or clasped in front. Nobody gestures, waves, greets, joins palms, bows, nods at the camera, adjusts clothing, walks, steps, enters or leaves. Exactly three people are in the frame from the first frame to the last, and nobody is ever hidden behind anyone. Nobody looks into the lens. Single continuous shot: no cut, no dissolve. Mouths stay closed; nobody speaks. No text, no logos, no readable screens.
